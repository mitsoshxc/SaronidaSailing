# Questions for the Owner

Things we need from the owner before or during the build. Answers go next to each question; move answered items into the docs.

## Domain, email and hosting

1. Where is `saronidasailing.gr` registered, and who has the login to the registrar?
2. Who manages the domain's DNS today?
3. Are there email addresses on the domain (e.g. `info@saronidasailing.gr`)? With which provider? (All email DNS records must be copied when DNS moves to Cloudflare, or email stops working.)
4. Does anything else use the domain (subdomains, other services, verification records)?
5. Who hosts the old site (the footer says Webmein.gr)? When does that contract end? We keep it running a few days after the switch, then it can be cancelled.
6. Is there access to Google Search Console / Bing Webmaster Tools for the site? (Needed to submit the new sitemap and watch the redirects.)
7. Who pays for hosting (DigitalOcean, about $6/month + $1.20 for weekly backups) and the domain renewal? Whose DigitalOcean and Cloudflare accounts should the site live in?

## Boat and trips

8. The old site describes a **Bavaria 44**; the new design is built around a **Lagoon 500**. Which boat(s) does the business operate now?
9. Boat details to confirm: cabins (number and layout), max guests per trip, engines, year. The design uses Lagoon 500 factory figures.
10. Prices: day cruise, islands trip, weekly charter, charter package, deposit, fuel.
11. Cancellation rules per trip type.
12. Is transfer from Athens offered? On request only, and at what price?
13. Exact departure point (for the Google Map pin) and boarding/return times.
14. Which booking platforms do you use? Their links for each trip.

## Contact and content

15. Email address to show on the site, contact hours, season (months).
16. Photos of the boat (exterior, cockpit, flybridge, saloon, cabins, trampoline, layout plan) and of the trips. Video is not planned for launch.
17. Review of the English texts (we will draft them from the Greek).

## Instagram

18. Switch `@saronidasailing` to a **Business or Creator** account.
19. Be available once, together with us, to create the free Meta developer app and press "Connect Instagram" in the admin.

## Legal

20. Company name, VAT number, registered address, insurance details, for the Terms and Privacy pages.
21. How long do you keep customer data, and where (e-mails, booking platforms)?
22. A lawyer should review Terms and Privacy before go-live.

## Admin access

23. Who will edit the site? An email per person; each needs an authenticator app on their phone (Google/Microsoft Authenticator).
